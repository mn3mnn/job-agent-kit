# Cover letter base

Cover letters should be **natural and short**: ~150-200 words, 4 short
paragraphs, plain first-person voice with contractions. The text goes into
the spec's `cover_letter.paragraphs` list (build_resume.py renders it in the
resume's style). Write one whenever the form has a cover-letter field or
upload, even if optional; also paste the same text into "message to the
hiring team" boxes.

## Rules

- Facts only from `cv.md`. No skill or number that isn't there.
- Paragraph 1 names the role and gives ONE concrete reason this team/product
  is interesting, taken from the job description (not "your innovative company").
- Paragraph 2 is the proof: 2 numbers from cv.md that map onto the job's top
  requirement. Swap in the closest-matching facts per job.
- Paragraph 3 is logistics: student status / location / start date.
- Paragraph 4 is one line. No "I am confident that...", no restating the resume.
- Be upfront about a real gap (e.g. "NestJS would be new to me") instead of
  stretching a fact.
- Banned: em/en dashes, arrows, "passionate", "leverage", "dynamic", "thrilled",
  "I am writing to express my interest", "fast-paced environment", "synergy".

## Template

> I'm applying for the {ROLE} role at {COMPANY}. {ONE SPECIFIC SENTENCE: what in
> the posting caught my attention and why it fits what I've been doing.}
>
> {PROOF: current/last role from cv.md with its strongest 1-2 numbers, then one
> earlier role with one number.} {OPTIONAL: one sentence tying a cv.md fact to
> the job's main requirement.}
>
> {WORKING STUDENT} I'm studying {DEGREE} at {UNIVERSITY}, so I can work up to
> 20 hours a week during the semester and more during breaks. I can start right away.
> {FULL-TIME} I'm based in {CITY} and can start {AVAILABILITY}.
>
> I'd be glad to talk it through. Thanks for reading.

Greeting: "Hello {COMPANY} team," unless the posting names a recruiter.
